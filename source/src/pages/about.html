How it works

From sign-up sheet to team list

Set up in minutes. Coaches need no account and no training.

1

Set up

Pick your sport and get its skills, positions and tests. Paste your roster or open the Excel file. Split it into groups and days.

2

Coaches score

They scan a QR code, type their name, and tap a score per skill by jersey number. No signal in the gym? Scores send when it comes back.

3

Pick your team

A combined ranking with a movable team line, player cards with strengths and work-ons, and a PDF to print or email.

Made for your sport

It speaks your sport

Every sport starts with its own words, skills, positions and tests. Change any of it, or start blank.

Features

Everything a tryout needs

Simple on the court, detailed when you are picking the team.

Score your way

By jersey number, by name, or one skill across every player. 1 to 5, 1 to 10, + ✓ −, or your own scale.

Run it together

Add as many organizers and coaches as you need. Everyone sees the same results, live.

Check-in

Mark players as they arrive. No-shows drop to the bottom and are skipped while scoring.

Stations

Give each coach their skills or groups. See who still needs scoring by whom.

Disagreement flags

See at a glance where coaches saw a player very differently, and compare coaches side by side.

Team decisions

Made team, waitlist or camp tracks in a tap, or filled from the ranking. A clean list to post.

Score guides

What a 3 or a 5 means, right under the score, so every coach scores the same way.

Days, weights and tests

Several days, weighted skills, and timed or measured tests in metric or imperial.

PDF and spreadsheet

Results, player cards and a full grid to print, email or open in Excel. Save your form as a template for next season.

After tryouts

Team picked? Track the season on ShotPulse.

ShotPulse is the game stats app from the same maker: shots, goals and saves by period, season totals, and a live link so parents can follow along. For hockey, ringette, basketball, soccer, lacrosse and handball.

Kids' data stays minimal and private

Number, name, position, division and group. No birthdays, emails or photos. Ratings are seen by the organizer only. Read the privacy page.
