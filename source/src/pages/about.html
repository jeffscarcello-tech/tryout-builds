How it works

Set up in minutes. Coaches need no account and no training.

1

Set up

Pick your sport and get its skills, positions and tests. Paste your roster from a spreadsheet. Split it into groups and days.

2

Coaches score

They scan a QR code, type their name, and tap a score per skill by jersey number. No signal in the gym? Scores send when it comes back.

3

Pick your team

A combined ranking with a movable team line, player cards with strengths and work-ons, side-by-side compare, and a PDF to print or email.

Made for your sport

Every sport starts with its own words, skills, positions and tests. Change any of it, or start blank.

Everything a tryout needs

Simple on the court, detailed when you are picking the team.

Score your way

By jersey number, by name, or one skill across every player. 1 to 5, 1 to 10, + ✓ −, or your own scale up to 1 to 1000.

Check-in

Mark players as they arrive. No-shows drop to the bottom and are skipped while scoring.

Groups and coaches

Give each coach their groups. See who still needs scoring by whom.

Several days

Average the days, add them up, or carry each score forward.

Notes on every skill

A quick note beside any score, plus an overall note per player.

Weights and tests

Weight the skills that matter most. Add timed or measured tests in metric or imperial.

Disagreement flags

See at a glance where coaches saw a player very differently.

Templates

Save your form and reuse it next season, for every age group.

PDF and spreadsheet

Results lists and player cards to print, email or open in Excel.

Kids' data stays minimal and private

Number, name, position, division and group. No birthdays, emails or photos. Ratings are seen by the organizer only. Read the privacy page.
