RosterPulse

Privacy

Updated November 19, 2026 · test version

The short version. RosterPulse is for coaches and organizers who run tryouts and camps. Players do not use it. We keep as little about players as we can, we have no accounts, ads or tracking, and we never sell anything. Ratings are seen only by the event's organizer unless the organizer chooses to share them.

What is kept about players

  • Jersey number, name (first name and last initial is enough), position, age division and group.
  • Whether they were checked in on each day.
  • The coaches' ratings, measured test results (for example a sprint time) and notes.
  • The organizer's team decision, if they record one (for example Made team, Waitlist or a camp track).

RosterPulse never asks for birthdays, emails, phone numbers, addresses, photos or location.

Where it is kept

  • On the organizer's phone. The event, roster, scores and notes are stored in the browser on that phone. They stay until the organizer deletes the event or clears the app. A backup file is a file the organizer saves and controls.
  • On evaluators' phones. A coach who joins an event gets the roster (number, name, position, division and group), the form and the check-in list, and keeps their own scores and notes. Private roster notes are never sent to evaluators. It is removed from their phone when they leave the event.
  • On other organizers' phones. If the organizer adds other organizers to an event, each of them gets the full event, including private roster notes and team decisions. Only the organizer who shared the event can add or remove them.
  • On our server, only for shared events. When an organizer shares an event, the server keeps the roster as above, the form, days, check-in list, evaluators' names, and every score and note, so the phones can stay in step. If there are other organizers, it also keeps the organizers' copy (the full roster with private notes) and the team decisions, which evaluators never receive. It is deleted when the organizer stops sharing or deletes the event, and automatically after 400 days without use. The server runs on Cloudflare, so information may be stored or processed outside Canada.

What we do not do

  • No accounts for coaches or players.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of information with anyone.
  • Nothing is shared with ShotPulse, our game stats app. The two apps are separate.

Like every website, the server sees the network address of each request. RosterPulse stores it only for a short time to stop abuse (for example, too many join attempts) and then removes it. Cloudflare, which hosts the service, keeps its own short-term logs for security.

Keeping shared events safe

  • Every connection is encrypted.
  • The organizer's key stays on the organizer's phone. Each evaluator's phone gets its own secret token.
  • Anyone with the 6-character join code can join as an evaluator, so organizers should share it only with their coaches. Organizers can remove an evaluator or stop sharing at any time.

For organizers

  • Make sure you have permission to evaluate the players, following your association's or club's rules.
  • Keep the roster to what you need. Do not put medical, contact or other sensitive details in notes.
  • Share results with care. A player's card, results list or decision list leaves RosterPulse only when you save or send it.
  • Add as organizers only the people who should see everything, and remove them when they no longer need it.

For players and parents

To see, correct or remove information about a player, contact the organizer of the event. The organizer can edit or delete it at any time; deleting an event or stopping sharing removes it from our server.

Changes and contact

RosterPulse is in testing. If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. A contact address for privacy questions will be listed here before RosterPulse launches.
